CodeRealm: The Python Chronicles - Game Rules

1. SETUP
   - Each player creates an account and joins a game room.
   - Download the physical board game map and QR cards from the Download section.
   - Each player starts with 5 Hearts (HP).

2. GAMEPLAY
   - Players take turns rolling the Digital Dice (1-12) on the web app to move their piece on the physical board.
   - Once the dice is rolled, check which tile you land on and click "Scan QR" to scan the corresponding QR card.
   - Start gives +5 Score and +1 Heart. Normal does nothing. Rest gives +1 Heart. These tiles do not ask a question.
   - Monster: correct +10 Score; wrong -1 Heart.
   - Treasure: correct +20 Score; wrong -1 Heart.
   - Trap: correct +5 Score; wrong -1 Heart and -10 Score.
   - Heal: correct +1 Heart; wrong has no effect.
   - Event randomly becomes Monster, Treasure, Trap, or Heal.
   - If a player reaches 0 Hearts, the game ends immediately and the final result is shown.

3. WINNING
   - The game ends when only 1 player is left standing (or if all players are eliminated).
   - Your final rank is determined by your total points.

Good Luck, Code Walker!
